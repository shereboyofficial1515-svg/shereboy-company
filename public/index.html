Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you ask.

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message and we'll reply personally.

How much does a website or application cost?

Every project is different, so we quote individually. After a short conversation about what you need, we send you a clear quote with what is included — no hidden costs.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the size of the project. A business website is much quicker than a custom management system. We agree a timeline with you before work starts and keep you updated throughout.

Do you only work with businesses in Warri?

No. We are based in Warri, Delta State, and work with clients across Nigeria and beyond. Most of our communication happens by phone, WhatsApp, email and video call.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Everything we build is designed for phones first, then tablets and desktops.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes, if you need to. We can include a secure admin dashboard so you can update text, images and other content without any technical knowledge.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer website maintenance and technical support, including updates, fixes and improvements.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you want to achieve. We'll recommend the right solution and give you a clear plan.
